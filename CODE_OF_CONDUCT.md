# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the Hypen community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers at ian@hypen.space. All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the reporter will be respected.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (the repository, Discussions, issues, pull requests) and when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.
